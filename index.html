How it works

Four steps. One complete campaign.

From a one-line description to posts, flyers, and reel videos — in minutes.

Step 01
✎

Describe

Tell gen8r what you're promoting — a wellness retreat, a new home listing, a restaurant opening, a yoga workshop. Set your event date and gen8r schedules backwards from it.

Step 02
⚡

Generate

AI creates a 10-piece mixed campaign: 6 posts with captions and images, 2 branded flyers, and 2 reel video scripts. All in 3-5 minutes.

Step 03
⚙

Review

Open the web editor. Pick your favourite images, tweak captions, approve reels to generate AI videos, or reject any piece.

Step 04
⚡

Publish

Set a start date. Each day you get a preview with the image — tap to publish to Instagram & Facebook with your brand stamp, or skip.

You Stay in Control
